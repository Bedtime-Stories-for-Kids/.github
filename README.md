## 🦔 THE HEDGEHOG AND THE LITTLE STAR — `LOST → FOUND → HELPED`

Once upon a time, in a quiet forest, there lived a little hedgehog. One night he heard someone crying in the grass. It was a little star—she had fallen from the sky and did not know how to get back home.

The hedgehog was not afraid. He gathered all his spines, made a little ladder out of them, and helped the star climb the tallest tree. The star smiled, flew up, and shone even brighter.

“Thank you, hedgehog!” she whispered. “Now I will always shine for you at night.”

And from that day on, whenever the hedgehog walked in the dark, one small but very kind star always glowed above him.

`LOST → FOUND → HELPED`

## ⭐ THE STAR WHO WAS AFRAID OF THE DARK — `FEAR → FRIEND → LIGHT`

At the very edge of the sky lived a little star. She was afraid of the dark and always hid behind the big clouds.

One day the moon flew over to her and said:

“Do not be afraid, little one. The dark is just a blanket under which the whole world sleeps. And you are its night light.”

The star peeked out from behind the cloud, looked down, and saw a little boy in a window who could not fall asleep. She shone as brightly as she could—and the boy smiled.

From that day on, the star was no longer afraid of the dark. She understood: her light was needed by someone.

`FEAR → FRIEND → LIGHT`

## ☁️ THE CLOUD LOOKING FOR HOME — `SEARCH → JOURNEY → HOME`

A little cloud was floating across the sky. It did not know where its home was, and it looked sadly down at the earth.

“Maybe my home is the mountain?” thought the cloud, and floated to the mountain. But the mountain was cold and silent.

“Maybe my home is the sea?” It floated to the sea. But the sea was loud and restless.

Tired, the cloud sat down to rest on a green meadow. And suddenly it heard:

“What a beautiful cloud! It looks like a little elephant!”

It was children playing in the meadow, pointing at the sky. The cloud smiled and stayed above the meadow. It understood: home is not a place—it is those who are happy to see you.

`SEARCH → JOURNEY → HOME`

## 🐻 THE BEAR CUB AND THE FIRST SNOW — `CURIOSITY → WONDER → WARMTH`

The bear cub had never seen snow. He slept all winter in his den and woke up only in spring.

One late autumn day he looked out of the den and saw white fluffs falling from the sky.

“What is that?” wondered the bear cub, and held out his paw. The fluff melted and became a drop of water.

“It is snow,” said mother bear. “It is cold, but beautiful. Soon the whole earth will be white, like a blanket.”

The bear cub watched the snow for a long time, and then said:

“I want it to snow every day. Then the world will be kind and soft.”

Mother smiled and hugged him. And the snow kept falling and falling—quietly and calmly.

`CURIOSITY → WONDER → WARMTH`

## 🌈 HOW THE LITTLE RABBIT LEARNED TO SHARE — `GREED → LESSON → FRIENDSHIP`

Once there lived a little rabbit who loved carrots very much. He had many carrots, but he shared them with no one.

One day a hedgehog came to him and said:

“Little rabbit, I have run out of food. Please give me one carrot.”

“No, it is mine!” answered the rabbit, and hid the carrots under a bush.

But soon a heavy rain came. The water washed away all the carrots, and the rabbit was left with nothing.

Then the hedgehog came to him again, bringing mushrooms.

“Help yourself,” he said. “I always share with my friends.”

The rabbit felt ashamed. He understood: when you share, the world becomes kinder. And from that day on, he always shared with his friends—carrots, mushrooms, and joy.

`GREED → LESSON → FRIENDSHIP`
